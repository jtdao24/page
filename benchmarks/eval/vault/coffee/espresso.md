# Pulling espresso

A standard double shot uses about eighteen grams of ground coffee and yields roughly thirty-six grams of espresso.

Aim for an extraction time of twenty-five to thirty seconds. A faster shot tastes sour, a slower one bitter.

Grind finer to slow a fast shot and coarser to speed up a slow one; change one variable at a time.

Use beans roasted within the last month, and let freshly roasted beans rest for about a week before brewing. See [[coffee/grinders]].
