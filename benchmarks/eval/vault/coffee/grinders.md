# Coffee grinders

Burr grinders crush beans to an even size, while blade grinders chop them unevenly, which leads to uneven extraction.

Conical burrs are quieter and cheaper; flat burrs give a more uniform grind but cost more.

Clean the burrs every month with a brush, or with grinder cleaning tablets, to remove stale coffee oils.

Single dosing, weighing beans for each brew, keeps beans fresher than storing them in the hopper.
