# Chain care

Clean and lubricate the chain every few hundred kilometres, and after every ride in the rain.

Apply one drop of lubricant to each roller, spin the pedals, then wipe off the excess; extra oil only attracts dirt.

Measure chain wear with a chain checker. Replace the chain at 0.5 percent stretch to avoid wearing out the cassette.

A skipping chain under load often means a worn chain or cassette; see [[cycling/tyres]] for the weekly checks to do at the same time.
