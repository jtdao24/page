# Bicycle tyres

Check tyre pressure every week; tyres lose air slowly even without a puncture. The recommended range is printed on the tyre sidewall.

Lower pressure gives more grip on rough or wet roads, while higher pressure rolls faster on smooth tarmac.

To fix a puncture, find the hole by pumping the tube and listening for hissing, or by dipping it in water and watching for bubbles.

Always run your fingers around the inside of the tyre to remove the thorn or glass that caused the puncture before fitting a new tube.
