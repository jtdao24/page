# Fixing a dripping tap

Most dripping taps are caused by a worn washer. Turn off the water supply under the sink before taking the tap apart.

Unscrew the handle, remove the valve, and replace the rubber washer with one of the same size. Take the old washer to the hardware store to match it.

If the tap still drips after a new washer, the valve seat is probably pitted and needs a reseating tool.

A slow drip can waste more than three thousand litres of water a year. See [[home/water-heater]] for other ways to cut water costs.
