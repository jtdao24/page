# Water heater care

Drain a few litres from the tank every six months to flush out sediment, which makes the heater noisy and less efficient.

Set the thermostat to about 50 degrees Celsius. Hotter water wastes energy and can scald; much cooler water risks bacteria growth.

Test the pressure relief valve once a year by lifting its lever briefly; water should flow and then stop.

Most tank heaters last eight to twelve years. Rust-coloured hot water is a sign the tank is failing.
