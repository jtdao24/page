# Exposure basics

Exposure depends on aperture, shutter speed and ISO. Changing one usually means adjusting another to keep the same brightness.

A wide aperture, such as f/1.8, lets in more light and blurs the background.

To freeze moving subjects use a fast shutter speed, around 1/500 of a second or faster.

Raise ISO only when needed, because high ISO adds visible noise. See [[photo/composition]].
