# Observing the planets

Jupiter shows its cloud belts and four bright moons even in a small telescope. The moons change position from night to night.

Saturn's rings are visible at around thirty times magnification. They tilt toward and away from Earth over a fifteen-year cycle.

Mars is best seen near opposition, every twenty-six months, when it is closest and brightest.

Planets shine steadily, while stars twinkle, which helps tell them apart with the naked eye.
