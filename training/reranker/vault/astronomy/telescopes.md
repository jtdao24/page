# Choosing a telescope

Aperture, the diameter of the main lens or mirror, matters more than magnification. A larger aperture gathers more light and shows fainter objects.

Refractors use lenses and need little maintenance. Reflectors use mirrors, give more aperture for the money, but need occasional collimation.

Let the telescope cool outside for half an hour before observing so the image stops shimmering.

Start with bright targets such as the Moon and the planets in [[astronomy/planets]].
