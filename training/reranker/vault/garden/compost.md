# Compost pile

A healthy compost pile mixes green material, such as grass clippings and vegetable peelings, with brown material, such as dry leaves and cardboard, at roughly one part green to three parts brown.

Turn the pile every week to add air. A pile that smells of ammonia has too much green material; add more dry leaves.

Finished compost is dark, crumbly and smells like forest soil. It usually takes three to six months.

Kitchen scraps come from [[kitchen/sourdough]] baking days and the vegetable drawer. Never add meat, dairy or oil.
