# Saving seeds

Let a few of the healthiest bean pods dry completely on the plant before picking them. The seeds should rattle inside.

Store seeds in paper envelopes in a cool, dark, dry place. Label each envelope with the variety and the year.

Test old seeds by rolling ten of them in a damp paper towel for a week and counting how many sprout.

The best tomato seeds come from fully ripe fruit; see [[garden/tomatoes]].
