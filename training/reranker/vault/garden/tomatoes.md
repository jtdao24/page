# Growing tomatoes

Tomato seedlings need at least eight hours of direct sun. Start seeds indoors six weeks before the last frost and move them outside once nights stay above ten degrees.

Water deeply at the base twice a week rather than a little every day. Wet leaves invite blight, so avoid overhead sprinklers.

Pinch off the suckers that grow between the main stem and the branches. Indeterminate varieties need a stake or cage before they start to sprawl.

Feed the bed with finished compost from the [[garden/compost]] pile when transplanting.
