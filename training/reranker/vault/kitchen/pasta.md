# Fresh pasta

Use one egg for every hundred grams of flour. Knead for ten minutes until the dough is smooth and springs back when pressed.

Rest the dough wrapped for at least thirty minutes so the gluten relaxes before rolling.

Roll the sheets thin enough to see your hand through them, then cut ribbons and dust them with semolina so they do not stick.

Fresh pasta cooks in two to three minutes in well-salted boiling water.
