# Sourdough bread

Feed the starter with equal weights of flour and water the night before baking. It is ready when it has doubled and a spoonful floats in water.

Mix flour, water and starter, rest for thirty minutes, then add salt. Stretch and fold the dough every half hour for the first two hours.

Proof the shaped loaf in the fridge overnight. Cold proofing deepens the sour flavor and makes scoring easier.

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 degrees with the lid on for twenty minutes, then uncovered until the crust is deep brown.
