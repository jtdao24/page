# Kyoto trip notes

Visit Fushimi Inari early in the morning, before the tour groups arrive, and walk past the first gates to the quieter upper paths.

A day bus pass covers most temples, but the subway is faster for crossing the city.

Many small restaurants take cash only, so carry yen. Convenience store ATMs accept foreign cards.

In autumn the maple leaves at Tofuku-ji draw large crowds; go on a weekday.
